07FAQs

Questions, answered.

Still have a question?

Send an enquiry and an admission counsellor will get in touch with you.

Ask a counsellor
Is Graphic Era Hill University recognised?

Yes. Graphic Era Hill University was established in 2011 under Uttarakhand State Act No. 12 of 2011 and is recognised by the UGC under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956. Its technical programmes are approved by AICTE.

Which campus can I apply to?

Dehradun, Bhimtal or Haldwani. Each campus has its own set of programmes; every programme in the Programmes section lists the campuses where it is offered.

Which programmes can I apply for?

70+ undergraduate, postgraduate and diploma programmes in engineering, computer applications, management, commerce, law, pharmacy, nursing, agriculture, English, media, design and hospitality. See the full list in the Programmes section.

What is GECET?

The Graphic Era Common Entrance Test is an all-India scholarship test by the Graphic Era Group of Institutions. Depending on your score, you can get up to a 100% tuition fee waiver.

Are other scholarships available?

Yes. Merit-based scholarships, plus additional support for female candidates and for wards of defence personnel. Students with Uttarakhand domicile get a 25% fee concession. The admissions team will confirm your eligibility for 2027–28.

How much is the application fee?

₹1,500, paid online with UPI, card or net banking when you submit your application.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes, on-campus accommodation is available. Ask your counsellor about room options and fees for your chosen campus.

Where are the campuses?

Dehradun: Bell Road, Clement Town, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248002.
Bhimtal: SatTal Road, Bhimtal, Nainital, Uttarakhand 263136.
Haldwani: Tularampur, Bareilly Road, Haldwani, Uttarakhand 263139.
